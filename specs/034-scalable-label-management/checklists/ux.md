# UX Requirements Checklist: Scalable Label Management

- [x] CHK001 - Are count and limit states defined below, at, and above the effective maximum? [Completeness, Spec US2]
- [x] CHK002 - Is the behavior of Add versus existing-definition controls explicit at the limit? [Clarity, FR-014]
- [x] CHK003 - Are desktop, tablet, phone, pointer, touch, keyboard, focus, and screen-reader expectations included? [Coverage, FR-015]
- [x] CHK004 - Is bounded scrolling defined without making heading, feedback, or Save/Cancel unreachable? [Measurability, US2]
- [x] CHK005 - Are palette exhaustion and repeated-color semantics separated from label identity? [Consistency, FR-016]
- [x] CHK006 - Are failure and stale-conflict draft-recovery expectations defined? [Coverage, FR-004 and FR-019]
- [x] CHK007 - Are both Board and card entry points required to remain behaviorally consistent? [Completeness, FR-013]
- [x] CHK008 - Is the impact warning for lowering the platform limit specified? [Clarity, US3]
